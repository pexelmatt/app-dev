# 🌊 Avatar: The Last Airbender 🔥

> My favorite animated series of all time.

## About the Show

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated series that aired on *Nickelodeon* from **2005 to 2008**. It follows Aang, a young monk who wakes up after 100 years frozen in ice and learns he must master all four elements to bring balance back to a world at war.

---

## The Four Nations

1. 💧 Water Tribes
2. 🌍 Earth Kingdom
3. 🔥 Fire Nation
4. 🌪️ Air Nomads

## Main Characters

- **Aang** – the Avatar and last airbender
- **Katara** – a waterbender from the Southern Water Tribe
- **Sokka** – Katara's brother, a non-bender with a boomerang
- **Toph** – a blind earthbender who invented metalbending
- **Zuko** – the exiled Fire Nation prince
  - His uncle, **Iroh**, is a fan favorite
